# Customer Quick-Start Guide

## Step 1: Onboarding
1. Navigate to `/register/customer`.
2. Enter corporate details, tax identity number, billing address, primary contact, and requested services.
3. Upload executed Master Services Agreement or Non-Disclosure Agreement.

## Step 2: Account Management
- Monitor account verification status on your **Customer Dashboard** (`/customer/dashboard`).
- View customer code (`CST-2026-XXXXX`).
- Raise support query tickets (`/customer/tickets`) for billing or service inquiries.
