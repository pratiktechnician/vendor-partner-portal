# Vendor Quick-Start Guide

Welcome to the Vendor Portal! Follow these steps to complete registration, upload compliance documents, and submit invoices for payment.

## Step 1: Complete 10-Step Registration Wizard
1. Visit `/register/vendor`.
2. Provide account credentials, legal company details, registered address, primary and finance contacts.
3. Enter PAN, GSTIN, and MSME category.
4. Input bank account details (account numbers are masked in general views for security).
5. Upload mandatory statutory certificates (Incorporation, PAN card, GST registration, Cancelled cheque).
6. Accept statutory compliance declaration and submit application.

## Step 2: Track Verification Status
- Monitor application status on your **Vendor Dashboard** (`/vendor/dashboard`).
- Receive notification alerts if corrections are requested by verification officers.

## Step 3: Submit Invoices
1. Once active (`VND-2026-XXXXX`), navigate to **Submit New Invoice** (`/vendor/invoices/new`).
2. Add PO reference number, select target department, and build line items.
3. Upload signed Invoice PDF and supporting GRN/completion signoff.
4. Track multi-tier approval progress and payout settlement real-time.
